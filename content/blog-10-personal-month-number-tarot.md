# Your Personal Month Number and Your Tarot Card

You might already have a personal month number. Numerology hands it over in two small steps. Add your birth month, your birth day, and this year, and fold the total down to a single digit: that's your personal year number, the theme the whole year is supposed to carry. Then add the number of the month you're in and fold again. Born March 15, and it's August 2026? The year folds to one, one plus eight is nine, and nine is your personal month. The idea is that the year sets the key and each month plays its own note inside it.

Tarot borrows the number the same way it borrows the life path. Take the single digit and match it to the Major Arcana card wearing it. Nine is the Hermit, so an August like that "is" a Hermit month, a stretch for drawing inward and tending your own lamp. It's tidy, and plenty of the tarot internet runs the month this way.

The problem is that when you fold down to one digit, you lose a bunch of the cards. I'll show you what I mean:

## Nine cards, then it starts over

A personal month number is always one through nine. So the card it points to is always one of the first nine Majors: the Magician, the High Priestess, the Empress, the Emperor, the Hierophant, the Lovers, the Chariot, Strength, the Hermit. Everything past the Hermit is out of reach. Temperance can't be your month. The Tower can't, the Star can't, the Moon can't. A single digit never counts high enough to arrive at them.

That's the same wall [your life path number](#) runs into, and it's [why reduction can only reach half the deck](#). But a month number does something a life path never has to. It climbs. Each month adds one and folds, so it steps forward through the nines and then loops. Run that same March 15 birthday across 2026 and the personal month lands on the High Priestess in January, the Empress in February, and one card further every month, up through Strength in July and the Hermit in August. Then September folds back to the Magician and the whole thing begins again. October repeats January, November repeats February. By the time the year is two-thirds gone you're walking a track you already walked. Twelve months, and only nine cards ever show up, some of them twice, and you never progress along the Fool's Journey to the latter half of the deck.

## The walk the Almanac reads instead

The Almanac keeps your birthday pointing to a card for the month and keeps the whole wheel intact. Instead of folding to a single digit, it sets the total on [the wheel of twenty-two](#): the Major Arcana laid in a circle, zero through twenty-one and around again, so the number lands on a position instead of collapsing. A month that runs past the end of the deck wraps around rather than shrinking, and the whole twenty-two stays in play.

Your month card still steps forward one place each month, so the motion you liked about the number is still there. It just steps around a bigger wheel. Take March 15 again through 2026. January is the Chariot, and every month moves one card on: Strength, the Hermit, the Wheel of Fortune, Justice, the Hanged One, Death, and by August, Temperance. Where the number folded back to the Magician in September, the wheel keeps climbing. September is the Devil, October the Tower, November the Star, December the Moon. Twelve months, twelve different cards, and no loop. The same August that the number calls the Hermit, the wheel calls Temperance, a card the folding could never have reached.

So the two methods agree on the shape of a year, a card a month walking forward, and disagree on the size of the room. One walks a nine-card loop and repeats before December. The other walks the full Major Arcana deck and lets a year touch Death and the Tower and the Moon, the cards the Fool's Journey spends its whole length climbing toward.

## Where the year card comes in

The walk has a starting point, and it's your year card. March 15 carries the Lovers for 2026, and the months step forward from there, one at a time, so the twelve of them read as one path rather than twelve separate pulls. Read the year card for the throughline and the month card for the turn of the wheel you're standing on right now. If you want the card the whole year is walking through, that's [your personal year card](#), and the months are the steps it takes.

The birth year isn't doing the quiet damage here that it does in a birth card, because a month card is meant to move with the calendar and the year belongs in it. What changes is only the folding. Reduce, and your months are a short loop of nine. Wrap the wheel, and they're a walk through all twenty-two.

## So is your personal month number wrong?

No. As a numerology signature it's doing its job: one digit for the flavor of the month, easy to carry, easy to check. If the Hermit fits your August, keep it. The strain only shows if you care about the full twenty-two Major Arcana cycle. Then the Tarot Almanac's got you covered. Add your month to the wheel instead of folding it, and see which of the full twenty-two you're actually standing on.
