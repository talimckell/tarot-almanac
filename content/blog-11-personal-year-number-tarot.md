# Your Personal Year Number and Your Tarot Card

Every January the same small sum goes around. Take your birth month, take your birth day, take the year you're walking into, and fold the whole thing down to one digit. Born March 15, and it's 2026? Three, then one and five, then two and zero and two and six. Keep folding until a single number is left. You get one. One is your personal year, the opening of a nine-year cycle, the year everything is supposed to begin.

Tarot picks it up from there. Match the digit to the Major Arcana card wearing it, and one is the Magician, so 2026 becomes a Magician year for that birthday: hands on the tools, something getting made. A lot of the tarot internet runs a year this way. One number, one card, twelve months of theme.

Then you do it again the next year, and the year after that, and the shape of the thing shows up.

## Your personal year number reaches nine cards, then repeats

A personal year number always lands between one and nine, so the card it points to is always one of the first nine Majors. The Magician, the High Priestess, the Empress, the Emperor, the Hierophant, the Lovers, the Chariot, Strength, the Hermit. That's the shelf. It's the same ceiling reduction hits everywhere, and it's [why reduction can only reach half the deck](#).

What a year does that a birth card doesn't is come back around. March 15 gets a one in 2026, a two in 2027, and climbs to a nine in 2034. Then 2035 is a one again. The nine-year cycle is the system's best feature and also its entire inventory: the same nine cards, in the same order, for the rest of your life. At fifty-two you're on the card you had at forty-three, and at thirty-four, and at twenty-five.

Nothing past the Hermit ever arrives. None of your years is a Death year or a Tower year. The Star doesn't come. Judgement doesn't come. The World, card twenty-one, the one the Fool's Journey spends its whole length climbing toward, is not among the things a personal year can hand you. [Your life path number](#) runs into the same wall, and a life path at least has the excuse of being one fixed signature. A year is supposed to move.

## The same birthday on the wheel of twenty-two

The Almanac keeps every piece of that sum and stops folding. It sets the total on [the wheel of twenty-two](#), the Major Arcana laid in a circle, zero through twenty-one and around again, so a number that runs past the end of the deck wraps instead of shrinking.

March 15 in 2026 comes out at the Lovers. Run the same birthday forward: 2027 is the Chariot, 2028 Strength, 2029 the Hermit, and then the decade turns over, the digits fall back, and 2030 lands on the Magician with 2031 on the High Priestess. One card a year while a decade holds, and a reset when it rolls.

Both methods circle. Nine years on, March 15 is back on the Lovers in 2035, the same way it's back on a one. What differs is the size of the thing circling. Walk one birthday across ninety years and the number visits nine cards, while the wheel visits all twenty-two. Death shows up. So does the Tower, and so does the World.

## Where your tarot year card actually comes from

Your year card is your own fixed card, advanced by the world's. The numerology version has no equivalent for this part, because a single digit has nowhere to keep the second half of it.

March 15's [Bearing](#) is the Moon, card eighteen, the card that birthday carries in every year there is. [The card everybody shares](#) in 2026 is the Wheel of Fortune, card ten. Eighteen and ten make twenty-eight, which wraps to six, the Lovers. That's the entire derivation: your Bearing, plus the world's number, on the wheel.

So the distance between your year and everyone else's year is your Bearing, and it never moves. In 2026 the world stands on the Wheel of Fortune and March 15 stands on the Lovers, eighteen steps around the circle. In 2027 the world stands on Justice and March 15 stands on the Chariot, eighteen steps around the circle. Same gap, new ground, every year you're alive, starting with [the one you were born in](#).

2027 does something odd with that gap. The world's card is Justice, eleven, and eleven is exactly half of twenty-two, so next year every person on earth stands directly across the wheel from their own Bearing, as far from it as the wheel allows. Whatever your birthday points to, 2027 sets you down on the opposite side of it. [The 2027 year card](#) has that year in full.

## What your year card does for the twelve months after it

A year card does more than sit there being the theme of the year. It's the position the twelve months walk out from. Your [personal month card](#) steps forward one place at a time from wherever the year set you down, which makes January the first step of a path the year already chose and December the twelfth.

Which is where the folding costs more than it looks. A year that can only be one of nine cards sets its months walking a short loop, and the loop closes before December. Give the year the whole wheel and the months inherit it.

## So is your personal year number wrong?

No. As a numerology signature it does exactly what it was built for: one digit, one theme, easy to carry and easy to hold up against your own life. If 2026 has felt like a one, keep the one.

The strain shows when that digit has to carry a tarot deck. Nine cards report for duty, thirteen never get called, and the nine you have are a loop you've already walked twice. If your years have started to feel like reruns of each other, the arithmetic is doing that.

Your next card is already sitting there. Stop folding, and go see which one.
