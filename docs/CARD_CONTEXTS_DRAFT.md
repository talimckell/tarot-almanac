# Card contextual angles — drafts for review

The scaffold is live but dormant: each card's JSON can carry an optional `contexts`
block with up to six angles, and the card page renders a "<Card> in a reading"
section (one `<h3>` per angle) only for the angles that have content. Nothing shows
until you approve and the copy is added to the card's JSON.

These are the high-search-volume reading questions the card pages don't answer yet
(the site already ranks ~#5 for "[card] how someone sees you" with no content).

**The six angles:** Yes or no · As feelings · As a person · As advice · Timing · How someone sees you.

**Process:** review and edit the copy below. Once you're happy with a card, I wire the
approved text into `content/cards/<slug>.json` and it goes live. These are first
drafts in your voice to react to, not final.

## The Chariot — DRAFT

**Yes or no**
A yes, and a yes that asks something of you. The Chariot says go, especially when the
question is about drive, travel, or pushing a thing through by will. It wants you to take
the reins and commit to one direction rather than wait for the conditions to be perfect.

**As feelings**
Strong and pointed. Someone feeling the Chariot toward you is sure of it and already moving
forward, more drive than softness. If they seem to be holding back, it tends to be about
control rather than doubt.

**As a person**
Driven and self-possessed, the one who sets a course and holds it. Focused, competitive,
hard to knock off track. At their worst they steamroll, treating people as things that sit
between them and the goal.

**As advice**
Pick one direction and commit. Stop letting yourself be pulled two ways. The Chariot wins by
harnessing forces that pull against each other and aiming them at a single goal, so take
hold of what you can control and move.

**Timing**
Fast. Expect things to come to a head in weeks rather than months, often around travel or a
decision you push through yourself.

**How someone sees you**
Capable and in command, someone who clearly gets where they are going. Driven, and a little
formidable. The risk is reading as all forward motion, so set on your direction that you can
seem hard to reach.
