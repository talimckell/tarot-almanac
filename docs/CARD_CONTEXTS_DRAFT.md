## Major Arcana (12)

### The Wheel of Fortune
- **Yes or no:** Yes, if the timing is on your side, and the Wheel usually says it is. Things are turning and a lucky break may be on its way. It doesn't promise how it ends, so be ready to adapt. Say yes to what's already moving and keep a backup plan.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Wheel toward you, their feelings are shifting, and so is the situation around them. A change in their circumstances may bring you closer, or they may feel carried along by events. Don't expect a steady line right now. Check in again once things settle.
- **As a person:** A person who rolls with change, often lucky or well-timed, who has seen real ups and downs. They're optimistic, adaptable, and quick to spot a chance. They can leave too much to luck, and they may blame bad timing for a decision that was really theirs.
- **As advice:** Say yes to the change that's already coming, and stop bracing against it. Do what's in your control, like paying the bills, doing the work, and showing up. Don't hold the highs or the lows too tightly. Both pass.
- **Timing:** Change arrives on its own schedule, but the Wheel points to a turn within weeks to a few months. A full cycle can take a year. Watch for small signs that things are moving.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as adaptable, lucky, and hard to pin down. You seem to land on your feet. Some may think you leave things to chance, so let people see the work behind your good timing.

### Justice
- **Yes or no:** Yes, if it's fair and honest. Justice says yes when the facts and your conscience both back you up. It says no when a decision depends on hiding something. It's a strong answer for contracts, legal questions, and anything about fairness. Read the details before you sign.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels Justice toward you, they're weighing things carefully. They care, and they also want honesty and fairness between you. They may be asking themselves whether it all adds up. They'll respond well to straight talk, so be honest with them.
- **As a person:** A fair-minded, honest person who thinks before deciding and values being straightforward. People trust them with hard truths. They can be strict, judging themselves and others by a high standard, and slow to forgive.
- **As advice:** Tell the truth, including to yourself. Look at the facts, read what you're signing, and take responsibility for your part. Make the decision that's fair instead of the one that's easy. Then follow through on it.
- **Timing:** Steady and procedural. Justice points to outcomes that take as long as paperwork and fair process take, usually weeks to a few months. Things move once the facts are on the table.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as honest, fair, and sharp. They trust you to tell it straight. Some may feel judged, so lead with kindness when you share a hard truth.

### The Hanged One
- **Yes or no:** Not yet. The Hanged One says to pause, because the answer will change once you see the question from another side. Wait, and try looking at it from the other person's point of view. If you've already waited a long time with no change, it may be time to let the question go.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Hanged One toward you, they're on hold. They may be unsure, stuck, or thinking things over before they act. They aren't ignoring you. Ask clearly what they need, and give them time.
- **As a person:** A thoughtful, unconventional person who sees things from an angle others miss. They're patient, open-minded, and willing to give something up for what they believe in. They can be indecisive, and they sometimes wait so long for the right moment that it passes.
- **As advice:** Pause on purpose. Stop pushing for a week and see what changes. Try the other person's point of view, or do the opposite of your usual approach. Let go of the outcome you've been gripping so tightly.
- **Timing:** Slow and on hold. The Hanged One points to a waiting period of weeks or a few months. Things start again once you change how you're looking at them.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as patient, unusual, and hard to rush. You may look like you're doing nothing. Tell people you're thinking it through so they don't read the pause as lost interest.

### Death
- **Yes or no:** Yes to ending something, no to keeping it the same. Death is a clear yes for questions about leaving a job, ending a relationship, or closing a chapter. If you're asking whether something can go on unchanged, the answer is no. It rarely means a literal death.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels Death toward you, something in their feelings is ending or changing. They may be letting go, or the relationship may be turning into something new. That could mean a clean break or a different kind of closeness. Ask them directly where things stand.
- **As a person:** A person who has been through major change and come out different. They're calm about endings, direct, and good at helping others through loss. They can cut things off quickly, and they sometimes leave before they've told anyone they were unhappy.
- **As advice:** Let it end. Name the thing that's already over, whether it's a job, a habit, a belief, or a relationship, and stop feeding it. Make the cut cleanly and kindly, then give yourself a few quiet days. Leave some room for what comes next.
- **Timing:** Often quick once the decision is made. Death points to an ending within days or weeks, followed by a quieter stretch of a few months before the new thing takes shape.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as intense, decisive, and different from before. You may seem to close doors without warning. Give people notice when a change you're making affects them.

### Temperance
- **Yes or no:** Yes, if you're willing to let it take its time. Temperance is a yes for things that grow through steady, moderate effort, like healing, a slow-building relationship, or a plan with a lot of moving parts. It's a no for anything that depends on rushing or going all in at once. Aim for the middle path and check back in a few weeks.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels Temperance toward you, their feelings are calm, steady, and growing. They like how easy it is to be around you and want things to build at a natural pace. They aren't trying to force anything, so don't read their patience as a lack of interest.
- **As a person:** A balanced, easygoing person who gets along with very different kinds of people. They're a natural mediator, patient and good at finding the middle ground. They can avoid taking a side when one is needed, and they sometimes keep the peace at the cost of saying what they really think.
- **As advice:** Slow down and find your middle. Mix things in small amounts instead of going all in on one thing. If you've been burning the candle at both ends, pick one thing to cut back this week. Healing takes the time it takes, so let it.
- **Timing:** Gradual. Temperance points to things coming together over weeks or months, sometimes a full season. Don't expect a sudden change. Check in on your progress once a month rather than every day.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as calm, fair, and easy to be around. You're the one who smooths things over when a group gets tense. People may assume you're fine with everything, so speak up when something really bothers you.

### The Devil
- **Yes or no:** Yes, if it's something you truly want and you know the cost. The Devil is a yes with strings attached. It points to desire, money, attraction, and ambition, and it asks whether you're choosing freely. If you feel like you can't say no, or you're hiding it from someone, treat the answer as a no until you've looked closer.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Devil toward you, the attraction is intense, physical, and hard for them to ignore. It can be thrilling. It can also be possessive, or tangled up with jealousy and need. Notice how they treat you once the excitement settles down.
- **As a person:** A magnetic, passionate person with big appetites and a clear sense of what they want. They're charming and honest about desire. They can get stuck in habits, people, or patterns they know aren't good for them, and letting go is hard.
- **As advice:** Name what has a hold on you. It might be a habit, a person, a job, or a worry. Ask honestly what you get from it and what it costs. The chain is looser than it feels, so pick one small way to loosen it this week, and tell someone you trust.
- **Timing:** Fast to start and slow to end. The Devil describes a pull that arrives quickly and can last as long as you let it, often months. Change usually comes within weeks of the moment you admit the pattern out loud.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as magnetic, bold, and a little dangerous in a fun way. You draw attention without trying. Some may worry about your habits and others may get pulled into them, so be honest about what you're really after.

### The Tower
- **Yes or no:** No to keeping things as they are, and yes to what comes after. The Tower says a plan built on shaky ground won't hold. If the question is about something you suspect isn't true, the answer is no. It's a yes to hard truths and to clearing out what was never solid.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Tower toward you, their feelings are in upheaval. Something has shocked them or a truth has come out, and they're reacting strongly. Don't take sudden outbursts too personally. Wait for things to calm down before you talk it through.
- **As a person:** An intense, honest person whose life has changed suddenly more than once, and who has rebuilt each time. They're resilient and blunt. They can blow up a situation, say things in anger, or bring chaos along with the truth.
- **As advice:** Let what's already falling fall. Don't spend your energy propping up something that's cracked. Get safe first by checking your money, your housing, and your people. Then rebuild on something true, one piece at a time.
- **Timing:** Sudden. The Tower points to a shake-up that arrives fast, often in days, followed by weeks or months of rebuilding.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as intense, direct, and maybe unpredictable. Your honesty can land as a shock. Soften the delivery when you can.

### The Star
- **Yes or no:** Yes, gently. The Star says hope is justified, especially after a hard stretch. It's a good answer for healing, recovery, and starting again slowly. The yes grows over time and it isn't an instant fix, so keep your expectations kind and your steps small.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Star toward you, they feel hopeful and tender. They find you calming and they can picture a good future. They may be healing from something themselves and they're glad you're around. The feeling is soft and sincere, so let it unfold at its own pace.
- **As a person:** A gentle, hopeful, kind person who has been through something hard and kept their faith. They inspire others and are generous with encouragement. They can be idealistic, and they may give away so much energy to others that their own tank runs low.
- **As advice:** Rest and refill. Do something small each day that restores you, like a walk, a glass of water, or a full night of sleep. Let yourself hope again, and take one small step toward what you want. Don't demand the whole recovery at once.
- **Timing:** Gradual and gentle. The Star points to a few weeks to a few months of slow improvement after a hard time. Watch for small signs, since they usually come before the big ones.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as calming, hopeful, and kind. Being around you feels like a breath of fresh air. Some may lean on you a lot, so protect your own rest.

### The Moon
- **Yes or no:** Not yet. The Moon says things aren't clear enough to answer. Details are missing, or fear is coloring how you read them. Wait for more information, notice what keeps coming up in your dreams and your gut, and ask again in a few weeks. Hold off on big commitments for now.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Moon toward you, their feelings are strong but confused. They may not know what they want, or they may be sending mixed signals. They could also be going through something private. Ask them one direct, kind question and give them time to answer.
- **As a person:** A sensitive, imaginative, intuitive person, often artistic, who picks up the mood of a room. They have vivid dreams and deep feelings. They can be moody, anxious, or hard to read, and they sometimes let fear fill in details they can't see.
- **As advice:** Slow down and don't trust your worst-case thoughts yet. Write down your dreams and hunches, and check the facts before you act on a fear. Go to bed early, or take a walk by water. Wait for the picture to sharpen before you make a big decision.
- **Timing:** Slow and unclear. The Moon points to a foggy stretch, usually a few weeks and sometimes a couple of months. Things get clearer a little at a time, so wait until you can see the next step.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as mysterious, sensitive, and intuitive. They may find you hard to read, and some will see you as moody. Tell people what you're feeling so they don't have to guess.

### The Sun
- **Yes or no:** Yes, one of the clearest yeses in the deck. The Sun says go ahead, especially for anything that makes you feel alive, like a celebration, a relationship, a child, or a creative project. Still read the fine print, since the Sun's confidence can skip past the details.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Sun toward you, they're happy about you and they show it. They're open, warm, and proud to be with you. There's very little hidden. Expect them to be direct about how they feel and to want to include you in their life.
- **As a person:** A warm, upbeat, generous person who lights up a room and makes others feel welcome. They're confident, playful, and honest. They can miss it when someone else is having a bad day, and they may push themselves to stay cheerful when they're struggling.
- **As advice:** Do the thing that makes you glad, and let people see it. Spend time outside in daylight. Share your good news and celebrate it. If you've been hiding or playing small, stop. Then check in with how you actually feel under the cheer.
- **Timing:** Fast and bright. The Sun points to good things arriving within days or weeks, and a stretch of momentum through the following season.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as friendly, confident, and fun to be around. Your energy lifts a room. Some may expect you to be okay all the time, so tell people when you aren't.

### Judgement
- **Yes or no:** Yes, when the question is about a fresh start or answering a real calling. Judgement is a yes once you've looked honestly at the past and you're ready to act differently. If you're only hoping the same pattern will turn out differently this time, wait and do that honest review first.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels Judgement toward you, they're having a real reckoning about you. They may be rethinking where they stand, owning something from the past, or realizing how much you mean to them. An honest conversation, an apology, or a fresh start may be on its way.
- **As a person:** A person who has looked hard at their own life and decided to change it. They're honest, forgiving, and often pulled toward a bigger purpose. They can be very hard on themselves, and they sometimes hold other people to the same standard of owning up.
- **As advice:** Take an honest look at the last year or so, then forgive yourself for what you find. Write down what you're ready to leave behind and what you want to do next. Answer the call you keep putting off, and take one step toward it this week.
- **Timing:** A turning point that arrives once you're ready, often within weeks to a few months. Judgement comes after the reflection is done, so the timing depends on how soon you finish looking honestly at things.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as someone who has grown, who is honest and willing to own things. Some may find you intense or serious. Others may still see the old you, so give them time and let your actions show the change.

### The World
- **Yes or no:** Yes, and the yes comes with a finish line. The World says what you're asking about will be completed or fulfilled, especially after long effort. It's a good answer for finishing a project, graduating, or making a big move. Remember the next chapter is already waiting.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the World toward you, they feel whole with you. They're satisfied, settled, and ready for something lasting. They see you as part of the life they want, and they're likely to say so.
- **As a person:** An accomplished, well-rounded person who has finished real things and knows who they are. They've often traveled or lived widely. They're settled and generous. They can coast on past wins, and starting something new after a big finish can be hard for them.
- **As advice:** Finish what's nearly done, and let yourself enjoy it. Mark the moment with a dinner or a day off. Then decide what comes next. Give this chapter a proper ending before you start the next one.
- **Timing:** Near the end of a long process. The World points to completion arriving in weeks to a few months. It's the finish line, so the hard part is usually behind you.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as capable, accomplished, and at ease with yourself. You seem like you have it together. Some may assume you have nothing left to want, so share what you're still reaching for.

## Cups (14)

### Ace of Cups
- **Yes or no:** Yes, especially for love, healing, and anything that starts from the heart. The Ace of Cups says the feeling is real and ready to open. If the question is mostly about logistics, the yes still holds, but don't skip the practical steps just because it feels good.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Ace of Cups toward you, something in them has just opened. It may be new love, tenderness, or simple warmth, and they haven't had time to shape it into a plan. The feeling is sincere, so be gentle with it while it's still new.
- **As a person:** A warm, open-hearted person who leads with kindness and feels things freely. They make others feel welcome and often start a conversation or a friendship without trying. They can be overwhelmed by their own feelings, and when they get hurt they may shut down completely for a while.
- **As advice:** Let yourself feel what's coming up, and say it out loud to someone you trust. Do one small kind thing for yourself or another person today. If you've been numb lately, start with something simple like a long bath, a walk, or a phone call to a friend.
- **Timing:** Soon. The Ace of Cups is a beginning, and beginnings tend to arrive in days or a few weeks. The feeling needs time to grow after that, so think of it as the first drop and not the whole season.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as warm, kind, and easy to open up to. They feel safe telling you things. Some may take your openness for more than you mean, so be clear about where you stand when it matters.

### Two of Cups
- **Yes or no:** Yes, especially for relationships, partnerships, and any agreement between two people. The Two of Cups says the answer is good when both sides want it equally. If you're the only one putting in effort, treat it as a maybe and check in with the other person.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Two of Cups toward you, the feeling is mutual and they know it. They respect you, they enjoy your company, and they want to be met halfway. They're likely ready for an honest conversation about where this is going.
- **As a person:** A fair, attentive person who treats the people close to them as equals. They listen well and give as much as they get. They can lose themselves in a partner, or hold back until they're sure the other person is as invested as they are.
- **As advice:** Reach out to the person in question and say what you actually feel. Aim for a fair exchange, where both people give and both people receive. If the balance is off, name it kindly now rather than letting it build.
- **Timing:** Steady and mutual. The Two of Cups moves at the speed of two people, so expect weeks rather than days. Things move forward as soon as both sides show up.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as fair, kind, and a good partner. You make others feel heard and valued. The risk is that you come across as too eager to please, so make sure your own needs get said out loud too.

### Three of Cups
- **Yes or no:** Yes, especially for anything involving friends, community, or celebration. The Three of Cups says this goes better with other people around. If the question is about something you'd have to do alone, it's more of a maybe.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Three of Cups toward you, they like having you around and see you as part of their circle. The feeling is warm and friendly, and it may not be romantic yet. They'd be glad to see you at the next gathering.
- **As a person:** A social, generous person who brings people together and remembers birthdays. They're fun at a party and loyal to their friends. They can spread themselves thin, or lean on the crowd so they never have to be alone with their thoughts.
- **As advice:** Call your friends and make a plan. Host something small, or say yes to the invitation you've been putting off. Spend your time on the people who are glad to see you, and let the rest go.
- **Timing:** Quick and lively. The Three of Cups points to good news and gatherings, often within days or a couple of weeks. Social plans move faster than most things, so say yes while the invitation is open.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as friendly, fun, and the one who brings people together. You make others feel included. If you start performing for the crowd, some people will notice, so let a few of them see the real you.

### Four of Cups
- **Yes or no:** Not yet. The Four of Cups says you aren't in the right frame of mind to answer well. Wait until you feel more interested, or look again at what's already on offer before you decide.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Four of Cups toward you, they're bored, distracted, or flat right now, and it may have little to do with you. They may be pulling back to figure out what they want. Give them space, and don't take the quiet as a final answer.
- **As a person:** A thoughtful, reflective person who spends a lot of time in their own head. They take a hard look at what they have, and they don't fake enthusiasm. They can get stuck in discontent, and they may miss what's being offered because they're busy looking inward.
- **As advice:** Take a step back and rest. Then look honestly at what's in front of you that you've been ignoring, like an invitation, an offer, or a person who keeps showing up. Try one small thing you'd usually skip.
- **Timing:** Slow. The Four of Cups points to a stretch of waiting and low energy, usually a few weeks or a couple of months. Things pick up once you start paying attention to what's already there.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as quiet, distant, and a bit hard to impress. Some may read you as bored or unavailable. If you do care, say so out loud, because people can't tell from the outside.

### Five of Cups
- **Yes or no:** Not right now. The Five of Cups says a loss is taking up most of your attention, and that's a hard place to decide from. Give yourself time to grieve, then ask again when you can see what's still standing.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Five of Cups toward you, they're hurting, and it may be about a loss that has nothing to do with you. They may feel regret or sadness and have little energy for anything else. A kind word helps more than advice.
- **As a person:** A deeply feeling person who takes loss seriously and doesn't pretend to be fine. They're honest about pain and good at sitting with others in theirs. They can get stuck counting what's gone and overlook what they still have.
- **As advice:** Let yourself grieve. Cry, talk to someone, or write it down. Once you've done that, take a look around for what's still standing, and take one small step toward it.
- **Timing:** Slow. Grief takes weeks or months, and the Five of Cups asks you to give it that time. Things start to lift once you turn around and notice what remains.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as sad, heavy, or hurting, and the ones who care will want to help. Some may not know what to say, so tell them what would be useful. Reaching out is the quickest way to feel less alone.

### Six of Cups
- **Yes or no:** Yes, especially for anything tied to family, old friends, home, or something you used to love. The Six of Cups is a gentle yes. If the question is about moving forward in a new direction, make sure you're not choosing it only because it feels familiar.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Six of Cups toward you, they feel fond and a little nostalgic. You may remind them of good times, or they may be thinking of reaching out after a long gap. The feeling is sweet and sincere, though it may be more about the past than the future.
- **As a person:** A kind, sentimental person who remembers everything and holds onto old friendships. They're gentle, generous, and good with children. They can live too much in the past and hold the present up against how things used to be.
- **As advice:** Reach out to someone from your past, or revisit something you used to love. Take what was good about it and bring it into this year. Don't try to go back and live there, because you can't.
- **Timing:** Gradual and gentle. The Six of Cups points to things from the past coming back around, often within weeks or a few months. Reunions and memories tend to arrive on their own schedule.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as kind, sweet, and a little nostalgic. You make others feel at home. If you keep talking about how things used to be, some may feel you're not fully here, so ask about the present too.

### Seven of Cups
- **Yes or no:** Maybe, or not yet. The Seven of Cups says there are many options and not all of them are real. Narrow the list to the ones you can actually do, and then ask again.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Seven of Cups toward you, they like you and they're also picturing a lot of other possibilities. They may be undecided, or daydreaming about what this could be. Don't expect a clear answer until they pick a direction.
- **As a person:** A creative, imaginative person with a head full of ideas and plans. They're inspiring to talk to and always seeing new possibilities. They can have trouble choosing, and some of what they promise stays in the daydream.
- **As advice:** Write down every option, then cross off the ones you wouldn't really do. Pick one and take one concrete step this week. A small real thing beats a big imagined one.
- **Timing:** Slow to settle. The Seven of Cups can stretch for weeks or months while you keep considering, and nothing moves until you choose. Once you commit, things speed up.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as creative, dreamy, and full of ideas. They enjoy your vision, though some may wonder if you'll follow through. Finish one thing and they'll take the rest more seriously.

### Eight of Cups
- **Yes or no:** Yes, if the question is about leaving, ending, or moving on from something that no longer fits. The Eight of Cups says it's time to go, even though it's hard. If you're asking whether to stay, the answer leans no.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Eight of Cups toward you, they may care about you and still feel something is missing. They're thinking about leaving, or they've already begun to pull away. It may not be about you, so ask them honestly what they need.
- **As a person:** A thoughtful, honest person who follows their values even when it costs them. They know when something is finished and have the courage to walk away. They can leave too quickly without saying why, and they may keep searching without finding what they want.
- **As advice:** Admit what's missing. If something is complete, or it no longer fits who you are, let yourself leave it. Plan the exit carefully, take what matters, and say goodbye kindly.
- **Timing:** A slow start and then a clear break. The Eight of Cups points to a decision that has built up over weeks or months. Once you walk, things change quickly.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as serious, self-aware, and a bit private. They may be surprised when you leave something that looked fine. Tell the people close to you why, so it doesn't feel sudden.

### Nine of Cups
- **Yes or no:** Yes, and it's one of the best answers in the deck. The Nine of Cups says you'll get what you wished for. Enjoy it, and still check that the thing you want is the thing you need.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Nine of Cups toward you, they're happy, satisfied, and comfortable around you. They enjoy your company and feel they have what they want. The feeling is content more than passionate, and it's real.
- **As a person:** A warm, generous person who enjoys good food, good company, and the good things in life. They're easygoing, proud of what they've built, and glad to share it. They can get complacent, or lean on comfort when something deeper is needed.
- **As advice:** Enjoy what you have. Say thank you, out loud, to someone who helped you get here. Stop chasing the next thing for an evening and let this one be enough.
- **Timing:** Fairly soon. The Nine of Cups points to a wish coming true within weeks, and the contentment lasts a good while after. Let it arrive without rushing it.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as happy, comfortable, and lucky. They enjoy being around you. Some may envy you or assume things come easily, so be open about the work that got you here.

### Ten of Cups
- **Yes or no:** Yes, especially for home, family, and long-term love. The Ten of Cups is a strong yes when it means building something lasting with people you trust. If it would cost the people you love, think again.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Ten of Cups toward you, they see a future with you. They think of you as family, or want to. The feeling is steady and committed, and it grows with time.
- **As a person:** A loyal, warm person who builds a home and keeps their people close. They value family, tradition, and peace. They can put too much weight on the picture of a happy life, and they may hide problems to keep it looking whole.
- **As advice:** Spend time with the people who feel like home. Make a regular ritual, like a weekly dinner or a Sunday call. If something at home needs a real conversation, have it, because peace lasts longer when it's honest.
- **Timing:** Slow and lasting. The Ten of Cups builds over months and years, though you may feel the first sign of it within weeks. It's the long-term outcome, so be patient with the process.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as happy, loyal, and deeply rooted. You make others feel like they belong. If things at home are harder than they look, let one trusted person know, so you're not carrying it alone.

### Page of Cups
- **Yes or no:** Yes, if the question is about something creative, new, or tender. The Page of Cups says give it a try and see how it feels. If a lot is at stake, make it a cautious yes and check your facts first.
- **As feelings:** If someone feels the Page of Cups toward you, they're sweet on you and a bit shy about it. The feeling is new, and they may express it through a message, a small gift, or a joke. Be kind, because they're probably nervous.
- **As a person:** A gentle, curious, creative person, often young or young at heart. They're sensitive, playful, and quick to follow a hunch. They can be moody, easily hurt, and unsure how to handle big feelings.
- **As advice:** Try the creative thing you've been putting off, and let it be imperfect. Pay attention to the small hunch or message that keeps showing up. If you're afraid of being rejected, ask anyway, and see what happens.
- **Timing:** Quick, in small ways. The Page of Cups points to a message, an invitation, or an idea arriving within days or a couple of weeks. Bigger results take longer, so stay patient.
- **How someone sees you:** People see you as sweet, imaginative, and a little shy. They find you easy to like. Some may think you're not serious, so show them a finished piece of work or a steady follow-through.

### Knight of Cups
- **Yes or no:** Yes, if the question is about love, creative work, or following your heart. The Knight of Cups says go for it. Before you commit, check that the feeling is steady and the plan is real.

## Pentacles (14)
